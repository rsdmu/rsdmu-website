Mushkani, R. (2026). **AI Pluralism and the Worlds It Misses.** ICML 2026 Pluralistic Alignment Workshop. arXiv:2606.16167.

The paper examines how technical categories can narrow what counts as evidence, harm, or benefit. It introduces Pluralistic Lifecycle Governance as a qualitative framework for documenting these choices and the authority to revise them.
