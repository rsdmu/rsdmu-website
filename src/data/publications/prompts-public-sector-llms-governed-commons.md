Mushkani, R. (2026). **Position: Prompts for Public-Sector LLMs Should Be Governed as Commons.** *Proceedings of the 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning*, PMLR 306, 171572–171582.

Prompt Commons treats the prompts used in public-sector AI as artifacts subject to public governance. The paper proposes versioned community repositories with records of origin, licensing, and moderation, and examines how stakeholder prompts can inform negotiated recommendations.
